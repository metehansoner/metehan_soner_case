Imposter Party is the hilarious pass-the-phone party game for friends, family, and game night chaos.

HOW TO PLAY
• Pick a mode: Classic or Drawing
• Choose from 16 fun categories
• Everyone gets a secret role — except one player is the Imposter
• Describe (or draw!) the word without saying it
• Vote to catch the faker

WHY YOU'LL LOVE IT
• Instant setup — no accounts, no waiting
• Perfect for 3–15 players on one phone
• Classic word play + drawing mode
• Beautiful clay characters & vivid party vibes
• Play in many languages

Great for parties, road trips, dorm nights, and ice-breakers. Download Imposter Party and find out who's lying!
